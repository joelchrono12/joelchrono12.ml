## Gaming

Gaming was pretty strong this month! I played a lot of videogames.

### Completed

- **Monument Valley** - This is a fantastic little game that I enjoyed playing through, a puzzle game that deals with perspective in fun and varied scenarios. There's a bit of a story going on, but everything is very minimal and up to interpretation. I remember really disliking this game just because it was very similar to *Echochrome* for the PSP, using perspective and illusions to create a path where there is none. But this adds a few new and interesting mechanics not present there, which I really enjoyed. Wonderful little game all around.

### Started

- **Outer Wilds** - What an incredible journey this game has been so far. I started it on a whim because my friend [JP](https://moddedbear.com) has been recommending it for ages, I finally gave it a try and now I'm instantly hooked. This is seriously incredible stuff. A game where you explore a small solar system, uncovering mysteries and secrets. It has been an absolutely joy so far.
- **Risk of Rain Returns** - I got this game to attempt to introduce my friends into another multiplayer roguelike that could be very fun to tackle together. Unfortunately we haven't been able to play it as much, and other options such as *Vampire Survivors* are all the rage right now for us.
- **Terranigma** - I wanted to play another retro action RPG and decided to give this one a go. It still plays and feels amazing! But I didn't play that much of it. I am charging up my retro handheld as I write this, I plan to continue playing more in February.

### Ongoing

- **Hollow Knight: Silksong** - I managed to make a lot of progress on this game, found a lot of bosses and routes and things to complete. It has been amazing to continue exploring the world of Pharloom even more, and the fact that there is so much more to explore in an upcoming act I'm yet to unlock fills me with a lot of excitement. Despite some very rough challenges and sections, being able to overcome all of that has made me feel great.
- **CrossCode** - On a single sitting, I managed to progress to the next dungeon of the game, doing a lot of sidequests and tasks before actually getting to it. The story has also started to unfold and turn out to be much deeper than expected. I've really enjoyed the characters here and all the quirky details that being "a game about playing a game" entails, like NPCs saying silly things and the like. It's fun!
- **Vampire Survivors** - Fantastic roguelike with a multiplayer mode that was simply awesome to play and experience together, I have to say I didn't expect it to take off as hard as it has, but it quickly became a new favorite in my friend group!
- **Slice & Dice** - This continues to be fantastic to play and get good at, I had an awesome Blursed run where I broke my record with 100+ consecutive wins. However, now I'm trying to beat Hard mode, which I have never been able to do. I should really try to improve my gameplay on that, I keep making silly mistakes.
- **Final Fantasy VI** - I only played a day or two of this, it was pretty fun but nothing much came out of it, I barely touched my RG35XX SP this month, I have to say.

## Manga

- **Kingdom** - The new arc against Riboku, the ultimate enemy of the Qin kingdom, has been absolutely thrilling, I'm just happily reading whatever the author throws at me, I know I'm in good hands, the action and the strategy never fail to impress me.
