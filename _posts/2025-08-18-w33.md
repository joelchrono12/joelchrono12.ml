## Watching

The thing this time is that I wanted to give *Alien: Earth* a watch. Right now, a new episode is coming out every week, and I thought I'd catch up on the Alien franchise after years without really paying much attention to it. Up until now, I had only watched the original Alien, Alien vs Predator, and Alien vs Predator 2. I somehow had never given the rest a go until this week.

- 👽🪖 **Aliens** - This movie was kind of awesome to watch even to this day. It's definitely a deviation of the pure dread of the original, but I still really liked the action scenes on this one, I was locked in for the whole thing, what a fun movie.  
- 👽3️⃣ **Alien 3** - Everyone hates on it, and the start definitely lets me see why, it's a bit of a bummer but at the same time, I miss the times when studios would dare to deviate so much and explore such random stories. Sequels today seem to be a bit too safe just to please the audience, this ain't one of those, and I kinda respect it.
- 👽🌍 **Alien: Earth** - I wanted to wait for the whole thing to come out, but I ended up watching the first two episodes and I had a blast. I loved the aesthetics and the world. The plot seems to be quite crazy too, although there are some weird "Disney was here" moments. Don't get me wrong, the horror and the action remain unscathed and aliens are just as terrifying as before.

## Reading

Not a bad week when it comes to reading, especially because Ariadne is a very cool manga, I want to catch up more on my books though, I could've finished by now

- 🗺️ **Ariadne in the Blue Sky** - Up to chapter 42. Yep I read more than 30 chapters of this series, and I have to say, it's pretty good! There is an ongoing mystery uncovering, as we keep exploring new corners of this world. I really enjoy the pacing thus far, there's always something interesting happening. A couple new characters have showed up, and even though the design is umm, quite revealing for no real reason, I am pushing through to see how this story evolves.
- 🛰️ **Nemesis Games** - Up to chapter 36. Yep, that's only one more chapter of progress, but I want to think all the manga reading helped balance it out. I can see it, the build-up paying off, and I hope everything turns out alright. Still wish I had made more progress honestly.
