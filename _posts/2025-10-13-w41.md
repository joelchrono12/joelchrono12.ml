## Watched

Not a lot of things were watched this week, but at least we went to the movies during the weekend!

- **Tron Ares** - I kinda liked this movie! I thought the soundtrack was awesome, and I quite enjoyed that it was basically a gian chase sequence most of the time. It does feel kinda incomplete though, and rehashes a bunch of concepts from Legacy, it's kind of a sequel to it while also being a replacemente, but it doesn't do the best job at either. ## Gaming

I am rather surprised at the gaming I did this week, given *Silksong* is not part of it. I feel kind of bad since I'm right at the last boss now, but at the same time, I did exactly the same thing to *Hollow Knight* for literal months, so it's within expectations.

### Completed

- **Hyper Light Drifter** - Finally played through the last bit of this game, after raising the four pillars, me and my friend took the time to find all of the modules and some of the other collectibles. After that, we faced against the final boss and beat it after many failed attempts, reaching credits at last. This game is a fantastic take on something akin to Zelda with just a bit of the Soulslike style, without getting too deep or complicated about it. I had a great time with it, the artstyle is immaculate, the music is incredible, I'll be writing a review soon.

### Ongoing

- **Soul Reaver Remastered** ✨ - This is October's game for *The Low Key Game Club* in the TWG Discord server, and I happen to be the one who suggested it. It is the reason I decided to pause my Silksong playthrough this week and played like 5 hours of this. This game came out for the PS1 back in the day, and the remaster brings new textures and character models, which look exactly how the game looked to me when I was a kid. I've managed to beat the first two boss fights, and I'm now exploring a spooky cathedral and solving puzzles to find my way around the place. Honestly, it's been quite enjoyable, despite some of the old school jank I've seen. The voice acting and the overall game design have been really awesome! I think I am pretty close to the last set of puzzles before I face against the next boss, we'll see!
- **Slice & Dice** - I am still blown away by this Roguelike's ability to stay interesting week after week. I think I should be writing a review soon.
