- 🏰 Because of [Dave](https://lategamer.bearblog.dev/), I ended up buying [Land of Eem](https://landofeem.com/), a whimsical and more colorful take on TTRPGs, that would end up with my frist time GMing (and playing at all) with some friends! This was an interesting experience and I am writing a complete blogpost about the whole ordeal, it was definitely the highlight of my week and I can't wait to share my thoughts about it. Spoiler: It was incredibly fun, and I have now ordered the physical deluxe set online...

I also ordered a new charger for my PSP, because I kind of lost my previous one somewhere.

## Gaming

- **CrossCode** - So I played like 8 hours of this game in a couple of days during my day off, and I finally got grabbed by the plot in a way I didn't quite expect. The dungeon was rather tough, full of challenging puzzles, but I eventually figured things out and had a lot of fun!
- **Hollow Knight: Silksong** - I continue to do everything I can to complete this game 100%. I had fulfilled a few wishes that I had missed and faced against some extra bosses and the like, super fun stuff.
- **Prinny: Can I Really be the Hero?** - Started this on a whim after charging up my PSP. This is a fun tough old-school platformer similar to *Ghosts & Ghouls*, I'm not sure I'll keep playing it but it was fun to test it out.
- **Nintendo Switch Sports** - Just a bowling match with friends after we were done with *Land of Eem*.
