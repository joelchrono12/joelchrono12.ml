Oh yeah here comes another media recap of everything that happened throughout the month of August. This was a very interesting time of the year. Filled with a lot of details you can find on my weeknotes. Here's an overview of how things went.

## Podcasts

This month I listened to 67 hours of podcasts! Pretty alright and normal to be honest.

- ✨ **Wolf 359** - Up to episode 24! Again, huge recommendation for this show. it is what got me into Podcasts in the first place many years ago.
- **Into The Aether** - This includes *Any Percent* and the new *Port Aether Short Leave* shoes in the Patreon feed. Awesome stuff!
- **Revolutions** - The French Revolution takes a very long while, I am still at it!
- **Avatar: Braving the Elements** - I've returned to this show to listen to Korra's and Zuko's VAs just doing their thing, interviews with different people who participated in the show, and retrospectives of every episode!
- **The Ten Minute Bible Hour** - The show finally reached the parables from the gospel of Matthew and it has been incredibly interesting to listen to the different ways to interpret the meaning behind them.
- **Talk Ville** - Clark and Lex talking about the Smallville show! (the actors, I mean), only listened to a couple, but they're fun.

## Movies

Movies were everywhere during August, the highlight is definitely the Alien franchise! I have talked so much about movies on my weeknotes that this time it'll just be the list and a super short summary. I even did a post specifically about [the Alien tetralogy](/blog/i-watched-the-original-alien-films/).

- **Aliens** - Alien but more of them, with soldiers and mechas.
- **Alien 3** - Aliens happy ending gets ruined, in a jail planet filled with prisoner zealots.
- **Alien Resurrection** - Alien but Ripley is cloned and has Alien DNA.
- **Prometheus** - A prequel to everything, it seems. Actually kind of liked it a lot!
- **Alien Covenant** - Alien before the original Alien. Seems to have received mixed reviews, but I still enjoyed it anyway.
- **Overcomer** - Just another meh christian sports film.
- **Narnia: The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe** - Best Lion and CGI than The Lion King's remake, epic soundtrack too, pure nostalgia.

## Anime & Shows

- **Alien Earth** - I decided to watch this on a whim and it is what triggered to just watch the whole Alien franchise while I was at it. I am really enjoying it, not as something like, fun, but it's intriguing and very interesting, filled with science fiction concepts everywhere, which I kind of like. I have a feeling that it has lots of mistakes or plotholes but honestly I don't care because it works for me.

## Books

- **Nemesis Games** - Almost done with this book, right now I'm on chapter 42, it has been a really thrilling journey, and everyone is in danger, and everything is in chaos, and I am pretty hyped as to what is going to happen next!s
- **All Systems Red** - This has been a new read I just begun. I bought the Martha Well's Humble Bundle and the Murderbot series was included, so I decided to give it a go! Only 2 chapters in, but I am enjoying it.

## Games

### Completed

- **Hollow Knight** - What if this if not the best indie Metroidvania Soulslike ever? or at least, until Silksong comes out? Review coming soon!
- **Jet Lancer** - Super bombastic arcade shooter which I really enjoyed and got hooked by, give it a go! [My review is up](/blog/jet-lancer)
- **Fire Emblem: The Blazing Blade** - Strategy RPG that goes straight to the point, with extremely epic animations I'll never grew tired of. [Review here!](/blog/fire-emblem-the-blazing-blade)

### Ongoing

- **Slice & Dice** - Extremely addictive and masterfully designed dice-builder roguelike. Totally loving it.
- **The Hundred Line: Last Defense Academy** - Progressed through my second run, some very fun moments, even romantic ones too, the fights are the most repetitive part now!
- **Ridge Racer 2** - Absolutely mesmerizing racing and drifting experience on the go, I finally embraced the first person mode too, you are not the driver, you ARE THE CAR!
- **Faster Than Light** - I tried a few runs literally yesterday, and had a lot of fun doing so. I really gotta get back into it.
- **Final Fantasy VI** - I tried just a couple of fights in the overworld and a mini-boss fight. Cool as ever, gotta play it more.
- **Advance Wars** - Returned to this little game and discovered that I was only playing the tutorial! Will definitely play more of the campaign now.
- **TMNT: Shredder's Revenge** - Just one evening of pure arcade beat'em up fun with some kiddos.
- **Mario vs Donkey Kong** - Decided to have this for those short moments where I can't really focus on anything else, fun levels!
- **Portal 2** - Still only playing co-op mode, aaaaaaah.
- **Marvel SNAP** - Just some matches here and there against online strangers.
- **Ultimate Chicken Horse** - Fun time with friends.

## Manga

- **Ariadne in the Blue Sky** - Up to chapter 80. Having a blast with this fantasy adventure, fun, chill and with pretty good fight sequences too. Liking it quite a bit.
- **My Wife is from a Thousand Years Ago** - Up to chapter 261 - Kind of a spoiler but the title already does so, they are officially married! Although there's no wedding event yet. 
- **Komi Can't Communicate** - Up to chapter 449 - Mostly just short stories with side characters since I went back to this, I hope it gets to the main couple again soon.
- **Usogui** - Up to chapter 63 - Quite a lot of action and fights for a gambling manga, I'll see what happens next.
- **Spy x Family** - Up to chapter 120 - The story telling of these side character stories has been superb and really emotional, I am up for these kind of moments over Komi's comic relief as of late.
- **Sakamoto Days** - Up to chapter 223 - It kind of feels like a finale for this Manga is coming soon, but I can't tell, I just know the action absolutely rips.
