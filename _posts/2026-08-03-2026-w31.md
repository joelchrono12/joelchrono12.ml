## Gaming

### Started

- **Star Wars Episode III (GBA)** - Well my sister decided she'd start this game after borrowing my Anbernic RG35XX SP for a bit, and I had to help her get through the first boss fight, only for her to give up soon after. I ended up picking this up and have made quite a bit of progress on it already! It's a beat'em up with lots of combos, unlockable moves and an actually great combat system for the duels, from what I experienced thus far in the boss fight against Count Dooku. Will probably have it in my rotation for a while before I finish it, or maybe I'll see it all the way through in a few days...

### Ongoing

- **Super Mario 3D World** - Me and friends finally returned to this awesome platformer. This time, we dedicated ourselves to collect more of the missing green stars and stamps in earlier levels instead of continuing with the stories. I just feel like we may as well! Given how good of a platformer this has been, and I don't really plan on revisiting it once I beat it once, so yeah. Super fun either way!

- **Fire Emblem Awakening** - I beat a couple of side quests to level up characters in my roster, but I made no progress on the main story this week. I did try and failed a couple times, as I lost some characters early on that I wasn't meaning to. In any case, I am hoping to get better and beat the chapter this week.

- **Donkey Kong '94** - This is also a reason why I slowed down on *Awakening*. This game is simply super fun! I have been doing world after world and now I'm at the 5th one, with a dozen levels for me to go through. I'm trying to get all the collectibles and most of the RetroAchievements, which makes for a wonderful incentive for me to keep going at it. Wonderful level and puzzle design!


#$# Completed

- ✨**Gris** - Early in the week I decided to give my Nintendo Switch some love, and I thought I'd play one of the games I chose for the Summer Game Challenge! I am extremely happy I chose this one. *Gris* is a wonderful and short experience, a platformer with light puzzle mechanics and an beautiful art style with pastel colors that really does look like a moving painting. It's just a wonderful experience that I'll have to write a review for soon. Rest assured, it's a good one.

## Watching

- **Masters of the Universe** - We saw this on Amazon Prime (it's a shared account I don't pay for btw) and kind of really enjoyed it! It was a very self-aware movie that really felt like a Saturday Morning Cartoon. I never watched the original He-Man, but I felt it would be a fun time. We ended up laughing quite a bit at how ridiculous it was, worth a watch I'd say.

- **Spider-Man: Brand New Day** - Now. No jokes here. This movie is Tom Holland's *best* Spider-Man film, and this is by a very, very long margin. The best Spider-Man film since the Raimi's *Spider-Man 2*. The character, the plot, the conflict at hand, everything was handled extremely well. This is also the best looking Marvel movie in ages, it feels like actual film done by people who know the character and truly had a story to tell. Of course, the action does not stay behind at all. The choreography and sequences were truly epic, everything here is top notch. Maybe my favorite film of the year. Even my parents want to watch it again in a couple weeks (because they are busy this weekend). It's that good.

## Reading

- **Leviathan Falls** - Chapters 12-31. That's about 20 chapters since last time I mentioned it! Since [Sotolf](https://sotolf.polymaths.page) told me I should read more books other than Manga, I decided I'd follow his advice. The last book from *The Expanse* has proven to be an absolute rollercoaster, with a lot of points of view from conflicting characters which are incredibly interesting to me. The story has started to collide and I can see the point of no return coming soon, I'm going to finish it this week!

- **Centuria** - Chapters 3-38. Well, this story sure managed to catch my intersting. Since the first couple chapters, that main character has met new allies, fought against a bunch of nightmare-ish creatures of the forest, rebelled against a whole kingdom, defeated a powerful enemy and losing another ally in battle. Quite a wild ride, so I'll keep reading for another bit. I made the mistake of looking up reviews and having people hating on it and affecting me but, I'll try to see it with my own critical view.

- **Uncle from Another World** - Chapters 26-27. The chapters of this one are much longer than the other, but actually reading my book also cut away some of my time here. In any case, a fun couple of chapters to entertain myself.
