## Reading

Reading is back on the menu, and I am super happy about that fact.

- **Persepolis Rising** - Up to chapter 40. I flew through 30 chapters in a week. At this pace I'll be able to finish this by next week, which is really awesome. This book started a little slow, but the pace has been going up, as well as my willingness to actually read for once, and I'm happy to be in a better headspace to enjoy it. The crew has been having a very rough time, but the tables are finally starting to turn a bit...

- **Planetes** - Up to chapter 16. Some of what I've read of this has almost made me cry. The stories and the way the plot has moved forward have been really thought-provoking, and this is really going up among my favorites, it's really, really good.

- **Spy x Family** - We are getting some fun action thriller stuff with Yor and Loid this time around, it's fun stuff so far and an enjoyable read. It's nice to go back to the adults and their shenanigans for once.

- **Blue Lock** - Up to chapter 337. Okay so the Japanese team is actually struggling this time, and France is not backing down. The mental conflict here has been very, very intense, as the values of Isagi and his team are being challenged like never before. I am loving this one so far.

## Gaming

I didn't touch *Grapple Dog* this week, and since I finished *Resident Evil 2*, I guess I just needed to laze around on the gaming side of things.

- 🏔️ **Peak** - A new game that I bought on a whim---to play with friends more than for the game itself---and so far it has been an incredibly fun experience. I played it a couple of times with [Jeremy](https://moddedbear.com) as well as with members of the TWG Discord and my time has been simply phenomenal. So much fun, so much chaos! Playing with my friends and trying to make it to the top. We even tried a mod to play as more than 4 people, and it went very well until the very last area where things only loaded up for 4 of us, and I was left behind. Still continued as one of us made it to the peak! Will keep playing it, it's just a nice way to chat and chill.
- 🥐 **CrossCode** - After [Amin](https://benjaminhollon.com) told me that he would finally play *Chrono Trigger* once I beat this game, I had to gear up and dust off my Nintendo Switch---I barely touched it at all this month. I only played around an hour because I was busy reading, but I had fun and really look forward to continue more of this adventure by next week. Apparently the plot will thicken quite a lot so, we'll see.
- 🏰 **Lost Castle** - I let myself be fooled into buying this roguelike 4-player game, but honestly, it wasn't that much fun, the variety was there for the enemies and weapons but it still felt samey to play, just dungeon crawling rooms and defeating goblins and all. This ended up being a bit of a meh impulse purchase that I may never touch again.
- 🧛 **Vampire Survivors** - Instead of that, my friends and I returned to this gem for a bit! We started the *Ode to Castlevania* stage and had an amazing time unlocking portals and a new character and similar things. We played rather well and almost made it to 30 minutes. However, we were rushing through enemies and got careless trying to unlock one more thing. Ended up dying with 30 seconds left for victory... Still had fun!
