## Reading

### Started

**The House on the Borderland** by William Hope Hogdson - Chapters 1-8. Since mentioning it on my post about [book translations](/blog/on-books-and-their-translations/), my interest was enough I thought I'd give it a go. I am actually reading this in both Spanish and English based on my mood and the paragraph, going through both translations and switching when some description gets confusing.

The book itself is one of those "lost book" types, where two men find this old journal that belonged to some old man in his solitary house. This work inspired H.P. Lovecraft and the cosmic horror genre, and it took me on an dreadful interdimensional voyage in less than two chapters. I recommend listening to the soundtrack of videogames like *Resident Evil* as you let the pages get a hold of your mind.

### Ongoing

**Hunter X Hunter** - Chapters 397-401. I finally returned to this awesome manga since I didn't realize they have started to release new chapters at last. I have to admit though, the story is getting a bit convoluted and I had to backtrack a few chapters to remember what was going on. There's a lot of characters doing a lot of things from multiple perspectives, genuinely like a dozen people protagonizing different parts of the plot. I enjoy seeing these characters again though, Kurapika is awesome.

### Completed

[**Clarkesworld Magazine #211**](/blog/clarkesworld-211/) by Neil Clarke - I finished reading all of the stories, and all of the non-fiction (interviews, essays and such) and thoroughly enjoyed this. It's the only Sci-Fi magazine I've read, but I already enjoy the format. My review is already up on my website.
