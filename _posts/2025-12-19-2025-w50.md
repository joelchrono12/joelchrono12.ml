Well, yesterday ~~and the whole week but I'm backdating and not expanding further~~ was an extremely busy day and I had zero time to actually write these, so back we go to writing and publishing these with a delay. Hopefully it doesn't happen next week, but no promises.

- ️🎙️ My [#AntennaPodEcho](https://fosstodon.org/@joel/115695849144448906) came out at last, and well, it's no surprise that Into The Aether made it, twice this time hehe.

- 🍔 My family got invited to by some friends to try out their burger business and they were absolutely delicious. I am not promoting or anything because it's a local thing, but yeah, that was some good food.

- 🎵 Got some music from Bandcamp! *Sun Kissed Lady* by Natsu Summer & Ryusenkei, who I have already listened to in the *Orange Airmail* album, really into it! Also, I bought the *Minecraft* soundtrack by C418.

- 🎁 Gifted my sister a *Kobo Clara Colour*! thankfully my family isn't very nitpicky with opening gifts on Christmas day, we just use them whenever we get them and pack them up for the photos once the date arrives.

- 🎶 Found [this neat tool](https://wrapped.devmatei.com/) to generate a graphic similar to *Spotify Wrapped* but with data gathered from your scrobbler of choice, Listenbrainz, LastFM, and even Navidrome.

- 🍕 With my sibling in town we all went together for some pizza on the weekend at the mall, we also invited a friend of the family and enjoyed our time together! We even played some card games publicly, laughing and throwing tantrums without any care of the people eating around us, it was kind of an interesting experience, I was self-conscious, but nobody actually cared much.

- 🃏 We finally had another match of *Betrayal at House of the Hill*, and well, we didn't finish, but the scenario seemed incredibly cool. It was just cat and mouse chase where we literally got shrunked down, and the traitor actually had some cats hunting us down! I really enjoyed what we managed to play of it, I wish we could have seen who won.
