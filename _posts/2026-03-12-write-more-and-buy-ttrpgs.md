My previous post ended with me mentioning how inspired I was feeling today, referring to my desire to make another post!

The truth is that this is often the case, especially on Mondays that are rather free of any work duties and where I am done with my weeknotes relatively quickly---even if there are times where I end up publishing them a day later, whoops.

The funny thing is that I don't really have any idea of what to write about, I just like to go blind and start typing something.

This was going to start out as a "What Interested Me Today" kind of post, and I was going to share links to stuff I found cool. But then I think *"I could just save these links for my weeknotes later"*, but I also remember I can just post whatever I want because it's my website and these are my rules.

I really like the format used on Steve's [Thursday Thoughts](https://lwgrs.bearblog.dev/thursday-thoughts-04/), where he shares his stream of thoughts throughout the day.

Honestly I just wanted an excuse to also mention that I just pre-ordered another TTRPG. I am talking about [Nimble](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/nimblerpg/monsters-and-more-a-nimble-ttrpg-reprint-and-expansion), which just reached a million bucks raised on Kickstarted and I contributed by ordering the complete physical set for it.

I haven't even received my physical [Land of Eem](https://landofeem.com/) set and I am already excited to get into more TTRPG systems for some reason.

I have some very bad influences that get me to buy evil things, but I am happy to be a bad influence to many people here as well, getting each other into expensive hobbies and ordering e-ink readers or whatever.
