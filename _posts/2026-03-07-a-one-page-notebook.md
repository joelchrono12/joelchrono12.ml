Last year I came across this super interesting article and for some reason I didn't even bring it on my weeknotes of that week. In any case, the idea floated around in my mind since then, but I didn't get around to it back then. It was just one of many things that seemed cool and productive, and I can't help but be a procrastinator for those.

A few days ago I saw [Alexandra](https://wrywriter.ca/) share the same post [on Mastodon](https://social.lol/@alexandra/116178253555891431) and it sparked the idea once again. It wasn't until today that things kind of clicked and I thought it would be fun to try it out!

I grabbed a piece of paper, folded it in 8 sections, cut in the middle to turn it into a little book, and started to write on it!

Some of the sections I added to mine this time around were:

- A cover
- A to-do list
- A list of media I liked
- A list of the food I ate today
- A random thought
- A random thing
- A how-to-make for the one-page notebook
- A doodle on the last page

A nice thing about this is that it's completely reversible! So you can have both sides of the sheet at your disposal.

This is how it looks when it's completely folded up. it's super compact and fits on any pocket, no big deal, and yet quite useful to have.

- Morning Breakfast: Eggs with chorizo
- Snack: Cheetos Flaming Hot

---

**Random Thought**

Even though I still make mistakes, I feel much more comfortable writing in cursive by now.

Feels much more fluid and I even forgot how some block letters are written, LOL

---

**More Random Things**

- Found an old speaker themed after the LSU Tigers, plugged my mp3 player and still works!! It had been unused for like 5 years.
- Got a  USB hub for my laptop, it's great!

---

**How to Make**

- Fold a paper sheet like so (in 8 parts)
- Fold in half and cut (on the short side, cut in the middle and just half, not all the way)
- Fold the other way now (on the long side)
- Expand and pull (folding it up as it opens)
- Done!

---

**This is the last page**
