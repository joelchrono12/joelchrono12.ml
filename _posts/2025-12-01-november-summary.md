## Manga

At least my craving for some Manga to read has been kicking up lately, it's been awesome to catch up on many series I was following, and to even complete something for once!

### Completed

- **Ariadne in the Blue Sky** - An incredible manga set in a fantasy world where our protagonists, a kid turned weapon of destruction, and a young princess from a floating kingdom, embark on a journey around the world to see everything it has to offer, many tribes and nations, a mystery to uncover and danger wherever they go. This story was amazing and I highly enjoyed it!

- **Erased** - A fantastic mystery manga, a sort of detective story with a time travel twist where the protagonist goes back 18 years into the past to stop a serial killer back then, and change his future as well! The reveals, the plot twists, it's a fantastic work and it even has a bit of that coming-of-age feel to it as well. Good stuff.

### Ongoing

- **Komi Can't Communicate** - Only 30 or so chapters left until I finish! This was the first manga I actually read and I wonder how it'll be to be done with it, so much has happened, the characters have gone through so much and I am honestly quite interested to so how everything ends.
- **Spy x Family** - Up to 122. I returned to this great series this month, and I had a great time! We've been exploring some new characters that are opening up the way to some interesting backstories and developments.
- **Sakamoto Days** - Up to 227.  Still going strong, decided to catch up a little bit. I feel like it has gotten a little weird as it's pretty clear nobody is in danger for the time being despite how impossible the odds are.
- **Frieren: Beyond Journey's End** - Up to 142. Now this is kind of thrilling, I've read the development of the latest arc where our protagonists act as bodyguards agaist some terrorist attack, and seeing how this develops has be biting my nails.
- **Blue Lock** - Up to 325. Just got back into this as well, catched up and eagerly waiting for the match against a pretty difficult team, France, or England.
- **Chainsaw Man** - Up to 220. Just as crazy and insane as ever, can't look away from this beautiful disaster. But really, how is it so good?
