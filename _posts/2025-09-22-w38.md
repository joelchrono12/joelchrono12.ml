I recently realized that I really like to read week notes. 

A few months ago, I kind of felt like they were filler, even as I wrote my own. However, there's something really special about the format. It is super basic and you know what to expect almost every time (depending on the writer). I've actually been seeking them more than regular posts just because they are more comfy; a slice of life from someone on the other side of the screen. 

When you read blog posts it's a bit less narrative and there's a need for more attention to the structure, the message, and the like (unless it's a slice of life kinda thing too). A weeknote is just describing stuff that happened! Unless your intro is a micro-essay or whatever.

This was a fairly long week, but we got through it. A lot of stuff happened, there are some pretty rough, sad moments, but also the every day life that just keeps going because there is no time to stop.

There was only one post between my weeknotes. Absolutely preposterous! And I had quite a few ideas and sparks, but they didn't manage to make it to the big screen---or whatever size the screen you're reading this post from---because of my lazyness.

- 🌮 My mom keeps preparing great lunch meals for me and I keep forgetting to bring them to work and she gets annoyed and I get annoyed at my own inability to just pick something from the table and put it inside my backpack.

- 🚨 There was an earthquake drill across the whole country! September 19th is a pretty significant date in Mexico, given the amount of earthquakes at that exact date, it's a bit of a bad omen. If nothing happens there's a drill anyway just to remember previous years, and stay alert at all times. I don't live in a dangerous zone, but it never hurts to get a 30 minute break at work.

- 🛍️ Went shopping again with family, last time we did this [I was quite melancholic about it](/blog/a-family-weekend-as-an-only-child/), but this time was much better. I got a birthday gift for my mom, and we had a great time together. I still missed my siblings though, but gotta get used to it. Besides, I bought a game for one of them, and a dinner for the other!

- 🗄️ I did a pretty exhaustive bedroom cleanup. Checking as many nooks and crannies---*I originally wrote "crooks" but I'm glad I looked it up*---I could find, throwing away a lot of trash---such as old packaging from the mail and things I bought---tidying up all the shoe boxes with boxes inside of them, relocating things and throwing away old clothes. The reason for all of this is honestly super random and childish. I simply want to find my *Hollow Knight* instruction manual and map poster that came with my physical edition of the game. So far, it's nowhere to be found, but I will not give up!

- 📦 I have been enslaved by package tracking numbers to know where stuff is going. The shirts I ordered a while back are on their way, as well as some YouTube merch I had ordered since May but never showed up (I sent an email and I was given the option to order the stuff again for free which was nice).

- 🎧 I forgot one of my earphones at home last Friday, so I only had one of them at work, however, due to the earthquake drill, I left both my Kobo reader AND the earphones case (with the single earphone inside) inside a drawer. I completely forgot about this so I ended up returning home and only having a single little earphone and no way to read my books for a whole weekend. Thankfully, I still kept my previous pair and they still worked rather well---after finally getting them to charge.

- 🛒 In yet another moment of weakness, I ordered like, a lot of enamel pins for a lot of stuff, from videogames, retro stuff and the like. I don't even use pins, maybe the reason I don't use pins is because *I don't have enough of them!*---or that's what my brain tells me. Will this change the course of my life? Maybe, maybe not, but at least I can gift them if everything goes wrong.

- 🕹️ The muOS developers finally decided to bless me with the [CANADA GOOSE update](https://muos.dev/release/current/canada), that fully fixed the stuck at boot issues for me, and it has also been the fastest installation time ever, absolutely loving this thing now, no issues so far. Next week will definitely feature some awesome retro gaming, after all this time, I know you missed it guys!

- 🕊️ A family we are friends with just lost their husband and father in a bike accident, his youngest child was riding in the same bike, he was miraculously unharmed. It was a sudden event that really changed how the weekend went for my family and my church. I hadn't gone to a funeral that involved people we were so close to in a long time. Death truly is the last enemy. Events like these make me think about a lot of stuff, and how I deal with those thoughts is something I may write soon. Rest in peace.
