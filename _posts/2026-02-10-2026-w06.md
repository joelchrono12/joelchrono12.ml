## Reading

- **Overscan** - As I mentioned on my [reading recap](/blog/reading-recap-2025/), I really want to write a review for this anthology that I completed last year, so I just reread it, and it was even better than my first read. I flew through the pages after I bought the digital edition. Writing a review this week, but rest assured, you should give it a go.

- **Persepolis Rising** - Read quite a bit more of this, but I am still very slow, I can't believe I'm the one keeping my book club behind! I never thought this would happen. I need to lock-in and rush through this book now!

- ✨ **Planetes** - Okay, this was a surprise. This Manga is INCREDIBLE, I started it on a whim and after just two chapters I was hooked. It's basically a sort of slice of life science fiction series with some astronauts cleaning up the skies from the debris of old satellites and space trash. Some great commentary and moments have been had, looking forward for more.

- **DanDaDan** - I finally caught up to this one! And the new arc has been rather interesting! We returned to a villain that has been teased for a while, and I'm interested to know how it develops.

- **Blue Lock** - I caught up again on the match between France and Japan, and it is a real challenge, Blue Lock can barely react now and all of their plans circling around Isagi have been stopped, a new evolution is required for victory to come.

## Gaming

- **Final Fantasy VI** - I've been making quite a bit of progress on this at last, recovering Edgar and the Figaro Castle, and making my way towards Setzer's quest to recover his wings! This should be very, very fun, as the world begins to open up for me once again.

- ✨ **Ninja Five-O** - This is a very interesting and fun game! I basically finished it on Easy Mode today, but I want to try Normal Mode too, which should include extra levels as well, that make matters quite interesting. At first it seemed like a rather average GBA title, but the simplicity shouldn't fool you, this game does have some really interesting mechanics, like a hook that lets you swing around, some stealth and kinda neat bosses.

- ✨ **Resident Evil 2** - This is the *Low Key Gaming Club* title for this month, and I decided to join in once again. I played a bit of it on my Anbernic handheld and it has been quite good! I need to keep playing some more though. I started with Leon's route, and I'm currently inside the police station.

- ✨ **Land of Eem** - I was told I should put this on the gaming section so here it will be now! This weekend we didn't actually play though, we spent a long while creating characters together and coming up with backstories and relationships! It was very interesting to actually learn how the system works better by doing everything ourselves.
