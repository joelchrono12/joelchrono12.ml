## Gaming

This week was crazy when it comes to games, but don't worry, in reality most of them were only played for an hour or less, except for *Slice & Dice*, *Ridge Racer 2* and *FTL*.

### Completed

- 🪡 **Hollow Knight** - I beat the game! reached credits and 100% completions. I pretty much only had to beat the boss, which took me like 20 minutes of attempts. I didn't beat Radiance and all that jazz though, but that's fine, I want to play Silksong. Review coming soon.

### Ongoing

- 🎲 **Slice & Dice** ✨ - Welp, this game just took over my life and is refusing to let go. Such a simple premise, executed to perfection, I shall play this game until the end of times. [*Thanks again Sotolf...*](https://sotolf.codeberg.page/posts/this-week-2025-34/), no but actually, I also have two other real life friends who are just as crazy for it, or even more than me. Apparently there are even mods and you can program your own characters and your own bosses and dice. My friend sent a custom boss and I managed to beat it, which was fun. I am mostly sticking to the classic mode though, and getting more and more achievements without even realizing it!

- 🏫 **The Hundred Line** - This game really got to the point where the battles aren't that interesting compared to the visual novel side of it. They kind of feel like puzzles that you already know the solution for, but you have to execute each step and it's kind of slow. However the latest one was a very interesting change, so maybe it's just a phase. We'll see how it goes!

- 👾 **Faster Than Light** ✨ - Did more than a couple runs on this one, for the same reasons as above. This game is incredible, but I suddenly got terrible at it in the end. Still, a fantastic gem you should try out!

- 🏎️ **Ridge Racer 2** - I think I kind of beat the game here too? I am still in the PRO courses and I reached the credits! I loved when the commentator sais that I AM THE RIDGE RACER. The next courses have been one on one races against special cars, and they've been quite challenging! After them I think I'll unlock an extra tour tier.

- 🌋 **Hades** - Literally just a short run that I started to see how I felt about addint it to my [Top 25 Games](/blog/top-25-games-of-all-time), but the interesting tidbit was the chatter I had with some characters, the fact that there's still a lot of plot to go through is just so interesting to me.

- 🎶 **Lumines Remastered** ✨ - I did a couple matches of this one, just to see how I felt about it when I added to my [honorable mentions](/blog/top-25-games-honorable-mentions), what a game this is!

- 🧙‍♂️ **Final Fantasy VI** -  After updating my Anbernic handheld to [MuOS Goose](https://muos.dev/release/current/goose) , I decided to go back to this game for a bit! I fought a boss and made some small progress. However, since the device didn't work well after the update, I don't want to play mure while the OS is unreliable.

- 🐴 **Ultimate Chicken Horse** - Just a few rounds of this party game with friends.
