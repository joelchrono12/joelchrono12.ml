## Gaming

This month I barely touched my Nintendo Switch, with my Anbernic RG35XX SP and my computer taking up most of my gaming time, for once! it's so weird because it's the same thing that happened [on February of last year!](/blog/laptop-gaming/)

### Completed

- **Resident Evil 2** - I didn't expect for this game to grip me in the way it did, but this classic of survival horror accompanied me during two straight weeks on my commutes to work, and I completed the Leon A and Claire B scenario right on time. [The review is up](/blog/resident-evil-2/) so feel free to check it out! Spoiler: I absolutely loved it and you should give it a try too. This game is so good it may steal a spot on my Top 25, not joking at all, many great mechanics and excellent replay value too.

### Started

- **Peak** - This is a very fun multiplayer game where you cooperate with friends to reach the peak of a mountain! You go through different biomes, find helpful items and try to not die or run out of stamina to keep going! I played with [JP](https://moddedbear.com/) for a couple sessions, as well as some friends from Discord.
- **Grapple Dog** - I had acquired this game last year in the a Humble Bundle and started it on a whim! I've done a couple of the worlds already and having a blast. It's a fun platformer with grappling mechanics, great physics and fun level design.
- **Prinny: Can I Really Be The Hero?** - I only really poked a this one for a second, but it's a tough as nails platformer similar to *Ghosts 'n Goblins*.

### Ongoing
- **CrossCode** - On the last day of the month, I played this one for an hour, which amounted to the most played Switch game of the month. I am making progress though and plan to focus more on it during March.
- **Ninja Five-O** - I completed all of the easy mode of this game, but I want to give it another go in Normal to access 3 levels that are unavailable to me, and see the true ending of it all.
- **Final Fantasy VI** - I made some more progress of this SNES classic at last, as I continue to find my friends again and plan to fight against the evil Kefka.
- **Donkey Kong** - Tried this gem of the original Game Boy, considered to be one of the best puzzle platformers of all time.
- **Nintendo Switch Sports** - I think we played this for like 15 minutes for a bowling match and then ran out of time.

## Manga

I enjoyed a great new manga this month, and continued on with some serials. The chapters of Planetes are longer than some other weekly manga, which is why the count seems low. In any case, good progress was made.

### Started

- **Planetes** - Read up to chapter 16. I had this one on my TBR list for a long time, and for some reason I finally decided to give it a go. This is a masterpiece, up there with *Yokohama Kaidashi Kikou* when it comes to thought-provoking---although not as comfy---manga to read and be engulfed by. A great piece of science fiction about the life of crew of space garbage cleaners in their day to day lives.

### Ongoing

- **Spy x Family** - Read up to chapter 129. Just a couple more chapters with a bit of a focus on comedy and action this time around.
- **Blue Lock** - Read up to chapter 337. The match against France has begun, and it is already shattering the way Blue Lock has worked so far, the french are giving them a run for it, and Isagi has been rather useless so far. I'm really interested to see how our characters evolve from here!
- **DanDaDan** - Read up to chapter 224. Really enjoying the progress on this one, and the way the new villain is approaching our protagonists is rather smart as well, I'm looking forward for more developemnt of this arc.
