Yet another week goes by, and the world keeps spinning. A lot of stuff happened, let's get through all of it.

- 🐕 My dog is doing much better now after the car accident---he is as grumpy as ever. However, he seems unable (or unwilling) to move a leg, and we haven't had a chance to take him to the vet again. He doesn't want to put weight on it, but he also doesn't react when we touch it or hold it. I'm worried it may have gone numb or something like that, we didn't notice this the moment we picked him up from the clinic because we were holding it the whole time, I would've brought it up immediately, can't believe I didn't think to check it, but even then we might have been told it would get better later. We'll see.

- ✉️ I received a mail from an online friend! it was a beautiful letter and a welcome surprise! I won't give names, but they know. Thank you so much! 

- 🏢 My co-worker went on vacation last week, and I had to cover for him for a couple of days. Thankfully nothing too bad happened and I got it all under control! Although I was still a bit stressed in the moment.

- 🚮 Did some volunteer work on the weekend and cleaned up some trash around the neighbourhood! There was quite a lot of plastic and glass bottles, caps, bags, and the usual, I forgot to wear sunscreen and I think I am now browner than normal. A lot of neighbors and some friends from church showed up and we were done in... not that much time!

- 📻 I've acquired quite a bit more music lately given my recent enthusiasm with [music albums](/blog/listening-to-full-albums-again), some of it was from Bandcamp, some of it was the result of "seeking within my soul" ;)

- 📁 Organizing music files (and files in general) is usually as much fun as actually listening to the music (or playing the videogame, or whatever equivalent. I ended up using [Picard](https://picard.musicbrainz.org/) to tag some FLAC files that were missing some metadata. Good stuff! 

- 🎼 Unfortunately, I've had mixed results with some japanese music metadata, because I prefer the name to be readable in English, but then ListenBrainz' scrobbling will not recognize the song properly---or it will get assigned to the wrong album. I may have to come to terms with just using the artist names in japanese characters.

- 🟩 I mentioned it on a [short updates post](/blog/short-updates/) but I've been playing some online word games (like Wordle) to pass some time and it's been really fun to engage on these again. I've returned to some like [Waffle Game](https://wafflegame.net), and discovered new ones! Such as [Tiled Words](https://tiledwords.com), which is my current favorite!

- 🃏 Played some *The Mind* with different people and the experience was quite awesome as well! Someone suggested to play some music and I thought "what better music to get in the zone than some *Balatro*?", so I just played a loop of that from YouTube. We managed to reach level seven!
