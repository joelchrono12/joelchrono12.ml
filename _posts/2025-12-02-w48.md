## Gaming

I actually didn't make much progress in most of the games I've been playing lately. The whole week has been dedicated to shorter multiplayer titles once again, it's been a fun change of pace, but I want to finish a proper game soon enough.

- **Mario vs Donkey Kong** -  This little gem of the GBA has been my main dish on my Anbernic handheld for the last couple weeks, as you may see. I finally completed World 3, the first one where I get all the high scores! I'll get them on the previous worlds and then more to the 4th. It has been super fun!
- **Nintendo Switch Sports** - Okay, it had been ages since I last played a match of Golf with friends, and this was a pretty crazy match. Yes, it's Golf, and it has it's boring moments in between. But when playing with friends those are the moments filled with banter and jokes or advice sometimes, and then the time to shoot came, and the anticipation and the build-up and the success and failures would provide us with lots of emotion. We played 9 holes and it was glorious. Also played volleyball and bowling, very fun too!
- **TMNT: Shredder's Revenge** - We played this a bunch and multiple days, sometimes with some people, some other time with others, I even tried it again with the friends I beat it with ages ago but in harder difficulty, we are on episode 4 so far, but we gotta git gud.
- **Super Mario 3D World** - Another nice amount of completed levels for this game, I am still getting all the stickers and stars before completing the last world though. It's super chaotic when playing with others, not gonna lie, but fun nonetheless.
