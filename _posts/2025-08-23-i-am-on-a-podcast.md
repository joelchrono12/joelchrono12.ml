Well, as the day winds down I thought I could share the secret I mentioned in my [previous weeknotes!](/blog/2025-w33/)

The title should be clear enough, you will be able to hear my wonderful English pronunciation for the first time ever, unless I've sent you a voice message sometime ago on Signal or you were one of the 6 or 7 people around that one time I joined someone playing Monster Hunter Freedom Unite on Twitch via a Discord call.

I participated in [episode 13](https://creators.spotify.com/pod/profile/brent-zeimen/episodes/Oh-No-Im-The-Old-Man-Again-Feat--Cassie--Joel--and-Cameron-e374bim) of The Worst Community Report podcast (here's the [RSS feed](https://anchor.fm/s/105981164/podcast/rss)) and I kind of dread to link it here. Still, I have to admit that I [expect some feedback out of it](/blog/blogging-expectations/).

For some context. The Worst Community Report is a variety podcast hosted by members of the community on Discord of [TWG](https://theworstgarbage.online/) (The Worst Garbage), which is kind of like a podcast network, mostly just in name and values, there are no ad deals or hosting services and such. [Into The Aether](https://intothecast.online), my most listened podcast show, is part of such a place.

Because of this, a fair amount of episodes can lack some context or talk about internal shenanigans that a 3rd party would be aware of unless they spend time in the server like I do. Overall, it has that "internet friends talking about random stuff" feel going for it, which I enjoy.

Honestly, I was super nervous all the time, and I haven't actually listened to it since the recording, I am so afraid of hearing my voice, I don't know if my small laughter showing up sometimes when others talked was left in, or that time I broke down midway trying to process what I was even saying lol. It felt like eternity to me, but maybe it wasn't that bad. Any reassuring words or honest feedback is welcome. 😅

This episode was centered on bloggers, and a few of the TWG Discord members participated. Including: 

- [Brent](https://tk-web.top/), the main host, who writes about Linux, FOSS and weeknotes. I've exchanged emails with him, leading to some cool conversations across a couple months now! I still don't reply back to the latest one oof.
- [Cassie](https://cassie.ink), who writes plenty stuff, from weeknotes, to blogging, books, technology and life in general. Also her website design is superb, absolutely lovely.
- [Cameron Swingle](https://thingle.pika.page/), who mostly sticks to game reviews, all of which are about super niche titles. I took a look at the [Top 25 Games](https://thingle.pika.page/posts/my-top-25-games-of-all-time-pt-i-25-21-the-nostalgia-zone) series, and only recognized like 8 of them, crazy stuff.
- and [me](/about).

We talked about how we got started, our blogging setups, and stuff like that, so if you want to, give it a listen! Even if you don't like my voice, maybe the topic will be enough to keep you interested.

This is it for now, have a good one!
