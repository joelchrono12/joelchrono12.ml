## Reading

My promise to read more has been fulfilled, I finished a book and returned to a magazine! I also read like half of the book of Joshua on my Bible, heavy stuff.

### Completed

- **Leviathan Falls** - Chapter 32-Epilogue. It is done, just as I promised, I managed to complete this book. The last of *The Expanse* series. Leviathan Falls tells us the grand finale of the Rocinante crew as they try to put an end to the greatest treat against Humanity as we know it. This was a very crazy journey with a lot of things going on. I am rather satisfied with the ending, though some things felt a bit messy, some questions were never answered, and things were a bit sad. The authors still managed to bring closure despite the huge stakes, and I am honestly very impressed by this. Now I don't know what should be my next big thing, ugh...

### Ongoing

- **Clarkesworld Magazine #211** - I decided to returned to this magazine, and finished three of the works included there. *The Rambler* by Shen Dacheng, *Occurrence at O1339* by Kelly Jennings and *The Oldest Fun* by Natalia Theodoridou. That only leaves the non-fiction articles. I am unsure of what to do about them since I kinda just want to keep on reading fiction. Should I just mark and finished and move on? I'll give a try to the first one and see.

- **Centuria** - Chapters 39-47. There have been some very interesting developments, an unexpected new friend for the main party and some nameless enemies for the hero to fight. There's a big unknown villain coming up so I'm interested to see that play out.

- **Blue Lock** - Chapter 355-356. England vs Japan is not going well for the Blue Lock team. The challenge is up and the stakes are high!
