Well, the month is over, and with that, Blaugust is gone as well! This is the second time I have participated on this challenge, and it has also lead to quite a lot of interesting interactions and a decent amount of posts.

To be honest, I was unable to reach the goal of 31 posts for the whole month. I wanted to give that a try and I even did two posts per day some times when I missed some previous days. However, as usual, some other matters got in the way! My siblings were visiting, my sister's boyfriend was visiting, and I also had a few days of vacation which I decided to spend on other hobbies unrelated to writing.

As such, I ended up with a total of 19 blogposts for the whole month, one less than July which didn't have an event tied to it! I basically wrote the usual, and well, that's pretty good, although my pacing was all over the place, with some weeks much fuller than others and the like.

Not many people can afford to write in the same way I do, so I am content, even if it's not a particularly impressive amount given my previous history.

Here is a quick list of some of my favorite moments and things that happened because of this event!

- I started chatting with Sam from [The Tangent Space](https://thetangent.space) about our blogging, videogames and specifically how epic Outer Wilds is.
- My post about [how we should react to slop](/blog/not-paranoid-but-respectful/) was one of many responses to [Andreas](http://82mhz.net/posts/2026/08/are-we-becoming-too-paranoid-about-ai-slop/), who had a fantastic Blaugust too.

- My post on [iconic gaming moments](/blog/iconic-moments-in-gaming) inspired Victoria to do a [tabletop edition!](https://gaudetetheology.wordpress.com/2026/08/16/iconic-moments-in-gameing-tabletop-edition)

- My post on [books and tranlsations](/blog/about-books-and-their-translations) inspired Marisable to write a [blog reply!](https://marisabel.nl/public/blog/RE_about_books_and_their_translations)

- [Jedda](https://notes.jeddacp.com) found this event very helpful to process her recent losses and grief.

- [Naty](https://burgeonlag.com) participated for the first time ever, and got a Silver Award just like me.

- [Syl](https://syls.blog) actually beat me this time around, I'm going to steal some of her post ideas for future dates.

And here are some of the post ideas I am yet to draft or post, which didn't make it in time for Blaugust!

- A very long review about Outer Wilds.
