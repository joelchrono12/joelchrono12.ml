## Reading

- **Komi Can't Communicate** - I have **finished** reading this! I am honestly kind of emotional about this. Komi-san was probably the first Manga I ever read a few years ago! It was a fantastic read for so long and I just kept on reading it for a few weeks and months. However, at some point, the story started to get convoluted with more and more characters and side stories, and the main point about Komi dealing with her communication disorder and trying to make friends with Tadano's encouragement took a backseat. Eventually though the relationship developed further and despite a few more hiccups, I think everything tied up quite nicely!

- **Spy x Family** - Read up to chapter 126. Oh, the current arc is a fun one, after some really effective backstory moments, the manga returns to some fun shenanigans with Anya that actually have higher stakes than what one may think. It's fun stuff!

- **Blue Lock** - Read up to chapter 327. The aftermath of Japan vs Nigeria was not what I expected at all, but it's cool so I don't mind to keep reading!
