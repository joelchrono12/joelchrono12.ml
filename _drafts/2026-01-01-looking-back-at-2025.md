Well, now that I have been living a pretty stable life with a regular job and living with parents, I can say that even such a normal samey day to day, comes with a lot of good things and surprising developments. This is a year where I got to try out many new things, found a few new hobbies, met a TON of new people online and in real life, and got to enjoy more time for myself and with family thanks to trips and vacations that I couldn't afford to have during my first year of work.

Overall, I'm pretty grateful with how everything turned out, and I wanted to review some of the things that I consider to be a highlight of the year.

- Offline music and listening habits
- More completed and played games
- Buying less videogames than in 2024
- More blogposts than ever before
- A bigger blogging community
