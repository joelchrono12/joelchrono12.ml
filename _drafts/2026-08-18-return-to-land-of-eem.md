Six months after the first campaign. We finally sat around the table once again, and played our first game of Land of Eem!

We had set a couple of dates before to meet up, but we had never been able to made it happen. After all, when a couple of party members are still of young age, you can only do so much! Plus, we could only align after church and sometimes other activities would follow instead.

This time some things are different, I purchased the Deluxe Edition Box Set, and I was able to use the official GM Screen, the map and the pysical core rulebook!

The rulebook comes with a starting adventure: **Wally's Waffles and Rats**, which is quite different from the one included in the Quickstart guide played last time. Spoilers ahead if you plan to play this with your own friends! But don't worry much, it will not go the same.

---

Well, first off, I went very much not ready for this. I had the character sheets, I didn't read them, I had all the rulebook, I didn't bookmark any important pages for reference during the game. I didn't remember a lot and honestly went straight into the game hoping for the game.

I did a half-decent introduction into the world of *Land of Eem* once again, focusing on the Used T'Be Forest and Wally's Waffle place. There were a couple of NPCs available to talk, but since Wally announces directly what will happen to this place and then talks to the party about it, my players kinda just ask him to let them check his basement.
