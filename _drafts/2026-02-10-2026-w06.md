I flew through the pages after I bought the digital edition. Writing a review this week, but rest assured, you should give it a go.

## Gaming

- **Final Fantasy VI** - I've been making quite a bit of progress on this at last, recovering Edgar and the Figaro Castle, and making my way towards Setzer's quest to recover his wings! This should be very, very fun, as the world begins to open up for me once again.
- **Ninja Five-O** - This is a very interesting and fun game! I basically finished it on Easy Mode today, but I want to try Normal Mode too, which should include extra levels as well, that make matters quite interesting. At first it seemed like a rather average GBA title, but the simplicity shouldn't fool you, this game does have some really interesting mechanics, like a hook that lets you swing around, some stealth and kinda neat bosses.
- **Resident Evil 2** - This is the *Low Key Gaming Club* title for this month, and I decided to join in once again. I played a bit of it on my Anbernic handheld and it has been quite good! I need to keep playing some more though. I started with Leon's route, and I'm currently inside the police station.
