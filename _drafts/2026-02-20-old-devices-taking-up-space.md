I've been cleaning up my bedroom---~~and this post is definitely not a way to procrastinate doing that~~---and finding a lot of things that I don't really use or need anymore, but at the same time, I don't feel comfortable just throwing them away. Some of them have no value for me whatsoever anymore, and some others are pieces of nostalgia that I won't throw away, but that technically fall under the definition of this post. They are devices that are currently collecting dust, shoved away in a drawer in my bedroom.

I still have my **Poco F4** and my **Mi 12T Pro**, both still charge and turn on, but eventually they'll start to bootloop because their power buttons aren't working properly.

Honestly don't know what to do with them, I could look into a repair shop to see if they can replace or clean up the hardware in some way, but for now they are still there gathering dust.
