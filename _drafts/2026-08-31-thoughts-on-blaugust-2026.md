Blaugust this year did not go as I expected it. I didn't foresee the amount of visits and activity around the house, I also didn't notice the amount of distraction I would have from YouTube's evil *Shorts*, and of course, the fact I chose to spend my time at home *not* writing did not help this cause.

Still, I am happy enough with the quantity of my blogposts, even if the pacing was all over the place. I believe I could have done better, but I refused to write on my phone for the most part, and I also had little to no deadtime at work, where I can often do a couple drafts when I'm feeling inspired.

In the end, I still managed to do 19 blogposts out of 31 (counting this one) and add a couple of interesting people to keep an eye on in my RSS reader.

A bit of a short reflection this time, I pretty much did the amount of posts I always do, after all, pretty dicent, but not what I intended. Time to move on, either way.
